## 2. What data we process

When you use the app without an account (as a guest), your records are kept only on your device and are not sent to us. When you create an account, the following data is processed:

- Identity and contact: email address; with Google sign-in, the name and profile photo link shared by your Google account.
- Customer transaction: user ID (UID), sign-in method, account creation date.
- Transaction security: IP address, device and browser information, sign-in times, a one-way hash of your password (kept by Firebase).
- Training records: plans, exercises, sets (weight, reps, level, range of motion), set and workout times, rest times, goals, app settings.
- Data that may qualify as health data (special category): sleep hours, effort and fatigue entries (RPE, RIR), information such as pain or injury that you may write in your notes, and the fatigue and recovery estimates calculated from them.
- Legal transaction: versions of the texts you accepted, explicit consent records and dates, correspondence about your requests.

## 4. How data is collected

Data is collected electronically: through the information you enter in the app, from Google when you sign in with Google, and automatically by Firebase Authentication while you use the Service.

## 5. To whom and why data is transferred

- To our service provider Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), as data processor, for hosting, authentication and sync. Firestore data is kept in Germany (Frankfurt); authentication data may be processed in Google facilities, including in the United States.
- To authorised public authorities and courts, only where required by law.
- To persons from whom we obtain legal support, to the extent necessary to protect our rights.

Because the data controller is established in the United States, when you use an account you send your data through the app directly to a data controller abroad. Where processing amounts to a transfer abroad, we rely on the appropriate safeguards provided in KVKK Art. 9 and the Regulation on the Procedures and Principles for the Transfer of Personal Data Abroad (a standard contract and its notification to the Authority). Your data is not sold or shared for advertising.

## 6. Retention period

Account and training data are kept while your account exists; special category data is kept while your explicit consent lasts. When you delete your account or withdraw your consent, the related data is deleted immediately. Details are in Section 8 of the [Privacy Policy](#doc:gizlilik).

## 7. Your rights under KVKK Article 11

By applying to the data controller, you have the right to: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it has been processed; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose; know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred; request its correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; request its deletion or destruction under the conditions in KVKK Art. 7; request that these actions be notified to the third parties to whom it was transferred; object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems; and claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.
