# Explicit Consent Notice

Version date: {{SURUM}}

This notice asks for your explicit consent to the processing of your data that may qualify as health data when you create a {{UYGULAMA}} account. Before giving consent, we recommend that you read the [KVKK Privacy Notice](#doc:kvkk-en) and the [Privacy Policy](#doc:privacy-en). If the English and Turkish versions of this notice differ, the Turkish version prevails for users in Turkey; for all other users this English version prevails.

## 1. Data we ask your consent for

- Your sleep hours,
- Your effort and fatigue entries (such as RPE and RIR),
- Information such as pain, injury or energy that you may write in your workout notes,
- The fatigue, recovery and load estimates that the app calculates from these entries and your training records.

This data may count as health data (special category personal data) within the meaning of KVKK Art. 6 and GDPR Art. 9.

## 2. Purpose

Only for the following purposes:

- Storing this data in your {{SIRKET}} account, syncing it between your devices and showing it to you,
- Using it in the app's fatigue, recovery and training load calculations.

This data is not used for advertising, profiling, sale, marketing with third parties, training AI models or any other purpose.

## 3. Where and by whom it is processed

The data is stored by our service provider Google Firebase in {{BOLGE}} on behalf of the data controller, {{SIRKET}}. The data controller is established in the United States; details and the safeguards applied are in Sections 6 and 7 of the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Consent is not mandatory

- If you do not give consent, no account is created; you can keep using every feature of the app as a guest. Your data then stays only on your device and never reaches us.
- Not giving or withdrawing consent has no other negative consequence for you.

## 5. You can withdraw your consent at any time

- In the app: Account › Withdraw my consent. Your cloud data is deleted, you are signed out, and your records stay on this device in guest mode.
- Or delete your account and all your data with Account › Delete account.
- Or write to {{EPOSTA}}.

Withdrawing your consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of your consent before it was withdrawn.

## 6. Declaration

When you tick the box in the app and tap "I give my consent", you make the following declaration:

> I have read and understood the Explicit Consent Notice. Of my own free will, I give my explicit consent to {{SIRKET}} storing my data that may qualify as health data, as listed above, in my account, syncing it and using it in the app's calculations for the stated purpose. I know that I can withdraw my consent at any time.

The date your consent was given and the version of this notice are recorded in your account.
