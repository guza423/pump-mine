# Account and data deletion

Version date: {{SURUM}}

This page explains how to delete your account and your data in the {{UYGULAMA}} app provided by {{SIRKET}}. You do not need to reinstall the app.

## 1. Delete your account in the app

1. Tap the account icon at the top right of the app.
2. Tap "Delete account".
3. If you signed in with email, enter your password again. If you signed in with Google, confirm your Google account again.
4. Type DELETE in the box and hold the "Delete account permanently" button for 1 second.

Your account and all your cloud data are deleted immediately, and the copy on your device is cleared. This cannot be undone. Before deleting, you can make a copy with "Export my data (JSON)".

## 2. Delete your account on the web

If you don't have the app at hand, open [{{WEB}}]({{WEB}}), sign in with the same account and follow the steps above.

## 3. Request deletion by email

Send an email with the subject "Account deletion" to {{EPOSTA}} from the email address registered in the app. Once we have verified your identity, we will delete your account and data within 30 days at the latest and let you know.

## 4. What is deleted

- Your account: your email address, user ID and sign-in details,
- All your plans, workouts, sets, notes and custom exercises,
- Your sleep entries, goals and settings,
- Your acceptance and consent records,
- The offline copy on your device and the 30-day safety copy made when you moved to an account.

## 5. What may remain for a while

- Technical records kept by Firebase for security (such as IP addresses) are deleted after a short period set by Google (a few weeks).
- Deleted data is permanently removed from Google's backup systems within their own cycles and is not used in the meantime.
- If you requested deletion by email, the correspondence showing that we fulfilled your request is kept for up to 2 years.

## 6. Deleting only your data, without deleting your account

- Individual records: open the record and hold its delete button.
- Cloud data: Account › Withdraw my consent. All your records in the cloud are deleted, you are signed out, and your records stay on this device in guest mode. Your account (your email address) remains; to delete it completely, follow the steps above.
- If you use the app without an account: Account › Delete data on this phone. Or uninstall the app.

If you run into a problem, write to {{EPOSTA}}.
